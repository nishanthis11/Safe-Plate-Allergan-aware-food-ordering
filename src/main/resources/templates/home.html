How SafePlate works

Three steps from sign-up to a safe meal.

1

Build your food profile

Add your allergies, diet, spice level and ingredients to avoid. You only do it once.

2

Browse with live checks

Every dish is compared to your profile, with clear warnings shown right on the menu.

3

Order with confidence

The server re-checks your cart at checkout, and the warnings are saved with your order.

Safety you can see

Four kinds of warnings keep you informed and in control.

KNOWN CONFLICT

The dish declares an allergen on your list. It cannot be added to your cart.

POSSIBLE CONFLICT

Cross-contact is possible, or an ingredient commonly carries your allergen.

DIETARY MISMATCH

An ingredient breaks your diet or is on your avoid list.

INFORMATION INCOMPLETE

The restaurant has not confirmed the ingredient and allergen details yet.
